# Shopify Landing Page — Direction

MOBILE FIRST. Keep copy punchy.

## Structure
1. **HERO** — SMiiLESTOOPiD. / [Stoopid Smiley artwork] / short sarcastic
   supporting line / SHOP DROP 001
2. **DROP 001** — Meet Stoopid Smiley. Introduce the character without a
   giant biography; let the artwork and attitude do the work.
3. **FEATURED PRODUCTS** — Hero Hoodie / Graphic Tee / Crewneck / Sticker
4. **BRAND MOMENT** — Life is weird. People are imperfect. Sometimes the
   smartest available response is basically: Smile. Stoopid.
5. **CHARACTER WORLD** — Hint that Stoopid Smiley isn't alone; more
   personalities arrive in later drops.
6. **EMAIL / COMMUNITY** — Invite people in without sounding like a
   corporate newsletter.

## Production route (working plan)
Print-on-demand (e.g., Printful/Printify) connected to Shopify, so products
print when ordered — no upfront inventory cost.
