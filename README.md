PROJECT NAME

SMiiLESTOOPiD.

IMPORTANT:
Always write the brand name together as:

SMiiLESTOOPiD.

Do not separate SMiiLE from STOOPiD when presenting the official brand name.


============================================
THE CORE IDEA
============================================

SMiiLESTOOPiD. is an original sarcastic lifestyle/clothing brand built around silly, imperfect, self-aware humor.

Think:

A blonde moment became a lifestyle brand.

It's not about being stupid.
It's about those ridiculous little human moments when your brain apparently clocked out and all you can really do is smile.

SMiiLESTOOPiD. shouldn't take itself too seriously.

It is funny.
Dry.
Awkward.
Sarcastic.
A little unbothered.
A little chaotic.
Comfortable with imperfection.

The humor comes first.


============================================
BRAND PERSONALITY
============================================

90s attitude
+
dry deadpan sarcasm
+
oversized modern streetwear
+
cute/weird original characters
+
nostalgic graphic merchandise
+
"whatever" energy

Creative inspiration/vibe references:

• 90s Clueless-era fashion energy
• dry Daria-like sarcasm, WITHOUT copying Daria
• Billie Eilish-inspired oversized fashion attitude
• Spencer's-style irreverent novelty energy
• retro sticker/mascot culture

These are mood references ONLY.

All characters, illustrations, wording and merchandise must remain original to SMiiLESTOOPiD.


============================================
THE ORIGINAL CHARACTER UNIVERSE
============================================

HERO CHARACTER:

STOOPID SMILEY

Stoopid Smiley is the recognizable lead mascot of SMiiLESTOOPiD.

The character should feel:

• simple
• expressive
• instantly recognizable
• sarcastic
• slightly weird
• imperfect
• funny without trying too hard
• capable of becoming a recurring icon across clothing, stickers, accessories and packaging

Stoopid Smiley should ultimately become one member of a larger original SMiiLESTOOPiD character squad.

Additional characters can be introduced in future drops.

Each character should have its own personality, expression, sayings and product possibilities.


============================================
THE GOLDEN MERCH RULE
============================================

Whenever possible, SMiiLESTOOPiD merchandise should combine THREE pieces into one unified design:

1. SMiiLESTOOPiD. branding
2. Character/artwork
3. Associated saying

Do not treat those as unrelated pieces.

BRAND + CHARACTER + SAYING = THE DESIGN SYSTEM


============================================
VOICE
============================================

The SMiiLESTOOPiD voice is:

Dry.
Blunt.
Deadpan.
Self-aware.
Playfully stupid.
Sarcastic.
Short.
Unexpected.

Avoid:

• motivational-speaker language
• inspirational clichés
• explaining the joke to death
• corporate language
• overly polished influencer language

The joke should often feel like the character barely cared enough to say it.


============================================

VISUAL DIRECTION
============================================

The artwork should feel like SMiiLESTOOPiD has its own little universe.

Prioritize:

• bold mascot graphics
• strong silhouettes
• simple recognizable facial expressions
• sticker-friendly artwork
• embroidery-friendly simplified versions
• screenprint-friendly graphics
• intentionally imperfect personality
• 90s-inspired palettes and styling
• merchandise that still works when oversized

============================================
SUSTAINABILITY DIRECTION
============================================

Where realistically available through suppliers, prioritize environmentally friendlier clothing materials and packaging.

Evaluate options such as:

• recycled-content packaging
• reduced unnecessary plastic
• durable garments rather than disposable novelty garments
• responsible material options
• consolidated/minimal packaging

Do not make environmental claims unless they can actually be supported by the supplier/product being sold.

============================================
NON-NEGOTIABLE CREATIVE RULES
============================================

1. Keep SMiiLESTOOPiD original.

2. Inspirations are vibes, not characters or designs to copy.

3. Humor beats explanation.

4. Don't make the brand overly serious.

5. Don't turn it into generic self-help.

6. Stoopid Smiley is the Drop 001 hero.

7. Brand + artwork + saying should function as one merchandise system.

8. Maintain the unusual capitalization:
SMiiLESTOOPiD.

9. Preserve the sarcastic 90s personality.

10. Future characters should belong unmistakably to the same universe.


============================================
MASTER CREATIVE BRIEF
============================================

When creating anything new for this project, use this instruction:

"Create an original design for SMiiLESTOOPiD. that belongs to the established Stoopid Smiley universe. Keep the aesthetic rooted in 90s nostalgia, dry deadpan sarcasm, oversized contemporary streetwear and deliberately imperfect humor. The result should feel funny, collectible and wearable rather than motivational or corporate. Maintain completely original character artwork. Wherever appropriate, integrate the SMiiLESTOOPiD. name, character artwork and its associated saying as one unified merchandise design."
